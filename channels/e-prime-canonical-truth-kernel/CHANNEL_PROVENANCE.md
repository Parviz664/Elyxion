# CHANNEL_PROVENANCE

## Evidence classes

### AUTHOR / OWNER FACT
Direct owner statement about E-Prime identity, scope, decisions or desired role.

### USER_SUPPLIED_ARTIFACT
A contract/spec pasted or supplied by the user.
Strong evidence that the artifact was used/supplied.
Not automatic proof that the user personally authored every line.

### PRIOR_ASSISTANT_OUTPUT
Useful for reconstructing audits, proposals and observed behavior.
Not owner intent by itself.

### RECOVERED_FROM_LATER_REFERENCE
A version/field known only because a later artifact names or supersedes it.

### GITHUB_ARTIFACT
Durable repository evidence.

### UNKNOWN
No sufficient evidence.

## Required distinctions

`SUPPLIED_BY_AUTHOR != NECESSARILY AUTHORED_BY_AUTHOR`

`ASSISTANT_AUDIT != OWNER_DECISION`

`LATER_SUPERSESSION_REFERENCE != RECOVERED_FULL_OLDER_BODY`

`ARCHITECTURAL_E_PRIME != CHAT_ARCHIVE_BRANCH`
