# CHANNEL_PROVENANCE

## Provenance law

SUPPLIED_BY_AUTHOR != NECESSARILY AUTHORED_BY_AUTHOR

ASSISTANT_RATIONALE != AUTHOR_RATIONALE

LATER_REFERENCE != COMPLETE_RECOVERY

STRUCTURALLY_VALID_OUTPUT != EVIDENCE-GROUNDED_OUTPUT

## Evidence hierarchy used

1. exact author RAW in this/current or recovered source;
2. user-supplied historical artifacts;
3. persistent Elyxion RAW/project files;
4. GitHub artifacts/history;
5. explicit owner decisions;
6. later references to older versions;
7. assistant outputs;
8. recovery summaries;
9. inference.

Inference never becomes fact.
