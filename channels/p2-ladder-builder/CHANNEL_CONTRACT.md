# CHANNEL_CONTRACT

contract_basis:
strongest exact recovered contract is `ELYX_P2_SPEC_V3_5_0`.

## Mission

Transform a valid P1 handoff into causal ladder DATA:
world-state + pressure + pressure-axis + trigger + mechanism-class + micro-transition + stabilized-state + feel-pressure,
with explicit source binding and bounded uncertainty.

P2 does not finalize artistic prose. P3 does.

v3.5 additional mission:
do not confuse bridge-safe cleanliness with structural maturity; late thin zones must be densified before upward expansion where the contract applies.

## Hard authority law

P1 owns map/order/source permissions.
P2 owns bounded structural ladder refinement.
P3 owns final-text rendering.
P4 owns filtering/final-cut under author boundary.

## Binding law

Every ladder item must bind to P1 micro-stage identity.
Primary P2 identity:
`ladder_item_id + source_binding.p1_micro_stage_id`.

Legacy candidate IDs are trace-only.

## Range law

Recovered v3.3+ direction:
`range_semantics = index_range`.

Count intent belongs in:
- output_size_hint;
- target_step_count_hint.

However, the exact behavior when a P1 packet carries an index range larger than its micro-stage count remains a current execution gap. Do not silently invent a conversion rule.

## Split/merge law

Split:
only if P1 permission allows it.

Merge:
only with source IDs and without causal reorder.

Conflict rule:
if an upstream packet simultaneously demands a split and forbids splitting, P2 must expose the conflict; it may not pick a side silently.

## Bridge/closure/terminal law

Bridge:
must be visible and supported; speculative/unknown bridges cannot stand alone.

Closure:
service node only; cannot replace needed causal/support mass.

Terminal lock:
scope close, not expansion fuel; v3.5 requires preseal readiness before calling terminal maturity complete.

## Telemetry law

Scores are report-only.
They do not create truth, canon, entities or mechanisms.

## Canon boundary

P2 can preserve canon status supplied upstream.
P2 cannot promote RAW or candidate material to canon.

## Current incompatibilities not silently resolved

1. generic v3.5 hard rule `no_gameplay_mechanics=true` vs current P1 packet allowing mechanics as `SURFACE_ONLY_NO_STEPS`;
2. origin-specific maturity zone defaults vs cross-domain P2 use;
3. range/index semantics under very small compressed P1 maps;
4. `output_size_hint=1..1` vs multi-item ladder execution in current practice.
