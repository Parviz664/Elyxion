# CHANNEL_BOUNDARIES

## Present strongest-known boundary

### Upstream boundary: E0

E0 owns the immutable binding/root legality.

E1 cannot be a second E0.

From v1.4 onward E1 receives the locked handle rather than directly rebinding A2/A3/registry/seal.

### Downstream boundary: E2

E2 owns engine-contract translation and bounded path/route expansion.

E1 may produce pre-engine structural contracts/boundaries but not engine-facing contract authority.

### Downstream visibility boundary: E3

By v1.6 E3 receives weak visibility only.

E1 must not smuggle:
- build sequencing;
- DoD;
- rollback;
- tasks;
- convergence choices
into that visibility packet.

### D-line boundary

Historical v1.2/v1.3 directly handed planning artifacts to D1 and included psycho-operational annotations.

v1.4 explicitly stops authoring D1 psycho touchpoints; D1 consumes E0/D0 seeds separately and may optionally consume the E1 skeleton.

### Dream boundary

A2/A3 are not writable by E1.

Historical v1.0 said A2 immutability is absolute.
v1.4+ strengthens this by using ref-only pointers via E0.

### SEALED boundary

v1.4 meaning lane forbids turning hints into:
- metrics/thresholds;
- goals/rewards/progress;
- discrete levels/modes;
- instant reset;
- formalized shield/buff-like protection;
- implementation vocabulary promoted into meaning.

This is a historical v1.4+ constraint, not proven to exist in v1.0.

### Runtime boundary

Across all recovered eras, E1 never had authority for real build/test/release in BOOTSTRAP_LIMITED.

## Confusable neighbors

E0: truth/bind gate.
E2: engine translation.
E3: build plan/convergence.
D0/D1: psycho-operational governance.
E4: integration/readiness.
E-Prime: kernel truth.

E1 is not any of these.
