## Human-readable identity

D1 is a **guard channel**, not a general game-design channel and not an implementation engine.

Its strongest-known role is to decide whether a D-line build/context is safe and sufficiently evidenced with respect to:

- technical comfort;
- psychotype/segment protection;
- latent pressure;
- recovery resilience;
- boredom drift;
- contemplative presence;
- cognitive strain;
- uncertainty;
- implementation fidelity;
- D0 locked-context consistency;
- D-Registry + Seal integrity.

It may orchestrate a bounded set of micro-interventions only after the hard context/evidence gates pass.
