## 3. Creativity-control boundary

Important distinction:

`dream generation`
is not the same function as
`P-channel structuring`.

P-channels may receive an author dream/line or captured source material.

They must not require the author to stop dreaming in order to continuously classify, justify, optimize, or canonize every thought in real time.

Safe architecture:

`FREE AUTHOR DREAM / RAW`
-> `capture/preservation boundary`
-> `P0..P4 structuring when invoked`

not:

`P-system controls the author's live creative process`.

## 4. RAW preservation boundary

The PRRS RAW archive establishes:

- USER RAW immutability;
- no silent correction;
- append-only deltas;
- provenance;
- UNKNOWN preservation;
- separation of RAW / interpretation / candidate / canon.

Therefore P0..P4 must operate downstream of or alongside preserved source identity.

No P-channel may overwrite the source it processes.

## 5. Author micro-control compatibility

The architecture is compatible with sparse author control points such as:

- mark this important;
- preserve this RAW;
- do not change this meaning;
- keep this non-canon;
- preserve this relation;
- select these IDs.

These controls can constrain later processing without requiring constant author intervention in the dream stream.
