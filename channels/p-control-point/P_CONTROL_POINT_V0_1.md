### 3.1 Domain boundary

P-channels are an AI/work architecture for structuring Elyxion thought and source material. They are **not automatically gameplay systems inside Elyxion**.

No P-channel may be silently converted into a mechanic, lore entity, player role, runtime component, or world object without a separate explicit bridge and authority.

## 4. Authority order

1. Direct owner correction or confirmation.
2. Exact USER RAW.
3. Original primary source material when present.
4. Versioned contracted architecture already admitted by the owner.
5. Assistant transcription or interpretation.
6. Assistant semantic organization.

A lower-authority layer may never silently overwrite a higher-authority layer.

## 5. Immutability

- Locked USER RAW is never edited in place.
- Corrections are stored as versioned deltas.
- Superseded forms remain in history.
- History is not rewritten to make the present architecture look cleaner.

## 9. No-drop / no-invent

For every material output, the system should be able to answer:

`Which input element(s) produced this output?`

Failure classes:

### UNSUPPORTED_CREATION

A material output appears without an acceptable input/source lineage.

### MATERIAL_LOSS

A material input disappears without an explicit, source-supported reason.

### SILENT_NORMALIZATION

An unusual, uncertain, incomplete, or ambiguous author element is silently repaired into a cleaner meaning.

### STATUS_PROMOTION_DRIFT

RAW, interpretation, or candidate material is silently treated as contracted or canonical.

### ROLE_ESCAPE

A channel performs transformation outside its admitted role.
