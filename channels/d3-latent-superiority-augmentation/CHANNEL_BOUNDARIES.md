# CHANNEL_BOUNDARIES

## Hard authority boundary

D3 owns:
latent augmentation search and selection only.

D3 does not own:
baseline truth;
locked context;
human-safety guard policy;
runtime execution;
release;
canon mutation.

## Upstream boundaries

### D0
D0 owns locked context and allowed adaptation zones.
D3 may only operate inside them.

### D1
D1 owns harm/overload/comfort constraints.
D3 may not waive them.

### D2
D2 owns the immutable baseline.
D3 may reference but never rewrite, shadow, reprioritize or semantically redirect it.

## Downstream boundaries

### D4
D4 is current primary consumer.
D3 hands off overlays/evidence/rollback/trace; D3 does not become experiment orchestrator.

### D5/D6
D3 may emit audio/visual overlay candidates if relevant.
No evidence grants D3 ownership of those domain channels.

## Historical E-boundary

v1.0-v1.2 did contain E-channel dependencies.
v1.3 explicitly removes all E* dependencies and blocks active E logic/refs.

Historical documentation may mention E routes because history must not be erased.
That is not an active E dependency.

## Canon boundary

A D3 overlay is not automatically canon.
A D3 PASS, even if valid, proves contract-level overlay admissibility under D3's gates, not owner canonization of world/game truth.

## Runtime boundary

No build/test/runtime/release operations.
No performance/fps/thermal claims from D3 contract execution alone.

## Evidence boundary

No evidence -> UNKNOWN/BLOCK as required.
No synthetic number may be presented as observed evidence.
