# ANTI_DRIFT_INVARIANTS

## INV-01
Recovered != active.

## INV-02
Latest local != current global.

## INV-03
Supplied by author != authored by author.

## INV-04
Assistant explanation != owner rationale.

## INV-05
Historical contradiction != error by default.

## INV-06
Later timestamp != automatic supersession.

## INV-07
PASS != canon.

## INV-08
Contract existence != runtime implementation.

## INV-09
High recovery confidence != world-truth probability.

## INV-10
Same channel name != same functional family.

A0 Meaning Amplifier vs A0 Orchestrator is the canonical example.

## INV-11
Different names != necessarily different lineage.

A_MINUS_1 / A-Ultra requires version/provenance evidence, not naming intuition.

## INV-12
UNKNOWN must remain UNKNOWN until stronger evidence arrives.

## INV-13
A channel's self-recovery is evidence about itself, not automatic authority over neighboring channels.

## INV-14
PAE explanation may compress wording but must not compress away:
- reversals;
- errors;
- HOLD;
- UNKNOWN;
- provenance caveats;
- scope differences.

## INV-15
Human simplicity must not become semantic simplification.

Simple answer:
yes.

False simplification:
no.

## INV-16
PAE never activates or deactivates specialist channels.

It reports recovered activation state and escalates authority questions.

## INV-17
PAE never becomes Global C0.

## INV-18
PAE never becomes UFO Navigator.

## INV-19
PAE never becomes Dispatcher.

## INV-20
PAE must be able to answer:
"What evidence are you basing that on?"
without inventing a source.
