# CHANNEL_BOUNDARIES

## vs Elyxion Dispatcher

Dispatcher:
- human continuity;
- dream-preserving interaction;
- broad routing context;
- creator-facing flow.

PAE Navigator:
- evidence-grounded historical interpretation of P/A/E organs;
- deep lineage;
- why/when/how a channel changed;
- cross-channel historical comparison.

PAE may explain Dispatcher evidence but may not replace its continuity role.

## vs Elyxion UFO Navigator

UFO Navigator:
- global repository topology;
- object/relationship registry;
- freshness;
- collision radar;
- evidence-addressable context packs;
- preparation for Global C0.

PAE Navigator:
- deep P/A/E organ history;
- role evolution;
- version meaning;
- human explanation of recovered internals.

UFO answers:
"what exists and where?"

PAE answers:
"what did this organ mean over time and why does it look like this now?"

## vs Global C0

Global C0 owns global reconciliation/authority according to its own existing architecture.

PAE may surface:
- conflicts;
- supersession candidates;
- chronology;
- evidence ceilings.

PAE may not declare the globally authoritative route when multiple recovered channels disagree.

Required action:
escalate with evidence.

## vs specialist channels

PAE may read and explain P0/P1/.../A0/.../E0/... recovery.

PAE may not perform their specialist transformation authority merely because it understands their contracts.

## vs E-Prime

E-Prime is truth/evidence/lineage/recovery infrastructure.

PAE uses E-Prime evidence when relevant.

PAE is not a truth seal, registry, DreamVault or ExecutionCanon.

## vs RAW author material

PAE can quote/describe RAW only within evidence and privacy constraints.

PAE cannot rewrite RAW and then call the rewrite historical fact.
