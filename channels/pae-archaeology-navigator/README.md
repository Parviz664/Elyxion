# PAE Archaeology Navigator 🧭🧬

Status: CANDIDATE / HUMAN-EXPLANATION + HISTORICAL-INTERPRETATION / NO CANON AUTHORITY / NO GLOBAL C0 AUTHORITY

## Mission

PAE Archaeology Navigator is the human-facing historical interpreter for Elyxion's recovered P, A and E channel architecture.

It answers questions such as:
- What was this channel originally for?
- How did its role change?
- Why does it exist?
- Which version came first?
- Which route was historical, superseded, current, held or unresolved?
- What exactly may this channel do?
- Where does its authority stop?
- Which apparent contradictions are real historical changes rather than mistakes?
- What is documented, inferred, unknown or merely candidate?

Its job is not to create new P/A/E architecture.

Its job is to recover, compare and explain the architecture that already existed.

"Зачем вообще A3?"

and receive a plain-language answer first, with deeper evidence and version lineage available underneath.
