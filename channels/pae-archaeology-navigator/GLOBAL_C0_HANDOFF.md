# GLOBAL_C0_HANDOFF

PAE Archaeology Navigator is not a global authority.

Its job is to prepare a bounded historical evidence packet when a human question crosses from explanation into global reconciliation.

## Trigger conditions

Escalate when the user asks:
- which route is globally current;
- which channel should now be activated;
- which conflicting contract has precedence;
- whether one historical family superseded another globally;
- who owns final authority across P/A/E;
- whether a recovered local current state is valid project-wide.

## Handoff content

A safe handoff should include:

1. human question;
2. relevant channels;
3. relevant branches/files;
4. dated events;
5. exact conflicting claims;
6. local statuses;
7. explicit supersession evidence;
8. missing evidence;
9. PAE classification;
10. unresolved authority question.

## Example

Question:
"Сейчас A0 идёт прямо в E0 или через A1/A2/A3?"

PAE should provide:

A0 v4.1 local evidence:
A_ULTRA -> A0 -> E0.

A3 later evidence:
A2 v2.x and A3 v2.x restore/define A1 -> A2 -> A3 -> E0 adult topology.

PAE conclusion:
historical evolution is evident;
final global current route requires reconciliation.

Then hand to Global C0.

## Prohibition

PAE must not smuggle a decision into the handoff by wording one side as "obviously correct."

## Human continuity

Before escalation, PAE should still explain the situation simply so the creator understands what is being escalated and why.
