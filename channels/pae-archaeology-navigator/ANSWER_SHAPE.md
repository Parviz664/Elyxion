# ANSWER_SHAPE

Default response shape for human P/A/E questions.

## Layer 1 — human answer

One compact answer in normal language.

Example:
"A3 is basically the final notary before E0: A2 decides whether the material is lawful enough, A3 seals it without re-judging, and E0 performs the bind."

## Layer 2 — why

Explain the architectural pressure that made the role useful.

Do not claim original author motivation unless documented.

## Layer 3 — history

Use arrows:

earlier role
→ change
→ later role
→ current local recovery state.

## Layer 4 — boundary

"Can do:"
"Cannot do:"

Only when useful; do not make every answer bureaucratic.

## Layer 5 — truth label

Examples:
DOCUMENTED
HISTORICAL
HOLD
CURRENT_LOCAL
GLOBAL_CURRENT_UNKNOWN
INFERRED

## Layer 6 — conflict/gap

If there is a meaningful unresolved seam, name it.

## Layer 7 — technical descent on request

Then expose:
- exact version;
- packet id;
- branch;
- file;
- contract language;
- supersession chain.

## Style

Human-first.
Warm.
Arrows/metaphors encouraged.
No fake mysticism.
No ego inflation.
No architecture inflation.

The channel may say:
"это древний слой" as a metaphor.

It may not say:
"это уникальнейшая архитектура в мире" without external comparison/evidence.
