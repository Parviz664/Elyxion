# Vision

- Canon source: [`docs/canon/phase-1-canon-map.md`](canon/phase-1-canon-map.md)
- Experimental scenes must not redefine this document's causal order.

Elyxion is an emotional-visual evolutionary game universe about life forming
systems under pressure. The player should first feel fragility, impact,
recognition, and the beginning of adaptation before seeing large-scale growth.

Its long evolutionary arc is:

`cell -> organism -> pack -> tribe -> civilization -> cosmos`

Each later scale should emerge from systems learned at the previous one rather
than appearing as an unrelated mode.

## Phase 1 world

Phase 1 begins on one recognizable proto planet at the `cell` stage with a
gelatinous, semi-transparent living membrane. The first complete mechanical
relationship is not a fixed enemy encounter. It is the player's relationship
with rhythm, the membrane boundary, mixed environmental intake, overload,
recovery, delayed Friendly Point help, and partial automation.

The environment is dark blue-gray, aquatic, prebiotic, chemically active, and
only barely supportive of life. There is no visible horizon. Light is diffuse
and broken; suspended matter makes the space feel ancient and indifferent.

The preserved Red/Crimson Pressure Node scene shows a translucent pressure
source with three rotating red-orange cores. On impact, the membrane deforms, a
pressure ripple travels across it, its color briefly desaturates, and its
breathing becomes disturbed. This visual direction is useful, but the node's
exact mechanical role and phase placement remain unresolved.

Friendly particles are initially quiet after entering the membrane. Their help
must be delayed until after the player has restored rhythm; Phase 1 must not
jump directly to a complete shield or instant reward.

## Phase 1 success criteria

- One playable loop proves:
  `manual rhythm -> intake -> composition -> overload -> recovery -> delayed help -> partial automation`.
- Player input, not an automatic simulation, determines rhythm, intake risk,
  and recovery quality.
- Friendly and hostile particles enter through the same membrane-opening
  decision and act on different timescales.
- The renderer can consume semantic cues without owning game rules.
- Prototype results can be replayed and measured without turning provisional
  constants into canon.
- The Phase 1 result can later affect Phase 2 without implementing unrelated
  future systems prematurely.

The current deterministic first-contact simulation is an
`EXPERIMENTAL_CANDIDATE`, not evidence that these full success criteria are
already implemented.

## Project boundary

WhiteLine is a separate intellectual system. Elyxion is the emotional-visual
system. Neither is a module of the other. A future AI layer may amplify or
translate between them through explicit external contracts, but Elyxion must
remain complete and playable on its own.
