# Evolution roadmap

The roadmap protects Elyxion's scale: each milestone should make one new living
relationship visible before adding another layer.

## Phase 1: first contact

`1 planet -> 1 membrane -> 1 friendly particle -> 1 Red Pressure Node`

- establish the ancient aquatic atmosphere;
- show membrane breathing, impact deformation, ripple, and desaturation;
- wake the friendly particle from dormancy into recognition;
- end with a subtle connection, not a complete shield;
- keep the encounter deterministic enough to replay and balance.

## Next membrane-system milestone

- expand internal particles into distinct roles: green main nodes, oval helper
  particles, narrow connectors, and turquoise transfer particles;
- make small particles circulate, connect nodes, and carry energy;
- introduce the Selective Membrane Stabilizer as a regulation center;
- let the Stabilizer create turquoise protective blocks;
- surround, neutralize, and dissolve red threats through a visible causal chain.

This milestone must grow from the Phase 1 friendly particle instead of replacing
it with an unrelated mechanic.

## Long evolutionary arc

1. `cell` — boundary, pressure, recognition, adaptation;
2. `organism` — specialized internal systems and coordinated survival;
3. `pack` — cooperation, conflict, and shared signals;
4. `tribe` — memory, roles, culture, and collective decisions;
5. `civilization` — infrastructure, institutions, and planetary consequences;
6. `cosmos` — interaction between mature worlds and large-scale systems.

The transition criteria for later stages are intentionally undecided. They will
be defined only after the cell-stage loop is emotionally and mechanically clear.
